Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

MedConsult ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal health information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under HIPAA and applicable privacy laws.

1 Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, date of birth, gender, and login credentials.
  • Health information (PHI): Medical history, symptoms, diagnoses, prescriptions, and appointment notes submitted during consultations.
  • Payment information: Payment is processed by Stripe. We store only a masked card reference and transaction status — never raw card numbers.
  • Professional credentials (doctors): Medical license number, specialty, government-issued ID, and bank account details for payouts, all encrypted at rest.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and session duration for security and service improvement.
  • Communication data: In-app messages, video consultation metadata (not recordings unless explicitly enabled and consented to), and support inquiries.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • Providing telehealth consultation services and appointment management.
  • Verifying doctor credentials and maintaining platform safety.
  • Processing payments and issuing refunds.
  • Sending appointment reminders, prescription notices, and service updates via email or SMS.
  • Complying with HIPAA, state medical board regulations, and other applicable laws.
  • Detecting fraud, abuse, and security threats.

We do not sell your personal data or health information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3 HIPAA Compliance

MedConsult is a covered entity under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Your Protected Health Information (PHI) is:

  • Encrypted at rest using AES-256-based Fernet encryption.
  • Transmitted over TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS).
  • Accessible only to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • Logged in an access audit trail per HIPAA §164.308(a)(1)(ii)(D).
  • Never disclosed to third parties without your written authorization, except as required by law or for treatment/payment/healthcare operations as permitted under HIPAA.

4 Data Sharing

We share data only with the following parties:

  • Your treating doctor: Health information you provide is shared with the doctor you book an appointment with.
  • Stripe: Payment processor — subject to Stripe's own privacy policy and PCI-DSS compliance.
  • Daily.co: Video infrastructure provider for telehealth video calls — no PHI is stored by Daily.co.
  • Twilio: SMS reminder delivery — only your phone number and appointment time are transmitted.
  • Legal authorities: When required by court order, subpoena, or applicable law.

5 Your Rights

  • Right of Access: Download a complete copy of your data at any time from Settings → Export My Data.
  • Right to Rectification: Update your profile information at any time from your account settings.
  • Right to Erasure: Request account deletion by contacting support. Note: we may retain records required by law for up to 7 years.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Contact support to restrict how your data is used.
  • HIPAA Amendment Right: Request corrections to your medical records by contacting your treating doctor or our support team.

6 Data Retention

We retain your account and health records for a minimum of 7 years from your last appointment, as required by most state medical record retention laws. Payment records are retained for 7 years for tax and compliance purposes. You may request deletion of non-essential data at any time.

7 Cookies & Tracking

We use session cookies strictly for authentication and security. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or third-party analytics that share your data with advertisers.

8 Contact & Complaints

For privacy inquiries, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@medconsult.com or via our contact form. You also have the right to file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services Office for Civil Rights at hhs.gov/ocr.
