Getting Started

For Patients:

  • Create an account and complete your profile
  • Browse verified doctors by specialization
  • Select an available time slot and book your appointment
  • Manage your appointments from your dashboard

For Doctors:

  • Sign up as a doctor and complete your profile with credentials
  • Wait for admin verification (usually within 24-48 hours)
  • Set your availability windows through the admin panel
  • Generate time slots and start accepting patients
